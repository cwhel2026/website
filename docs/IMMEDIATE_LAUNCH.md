# Immediate launch checklist

Launch target: as soon as operationally ready, not March 2027.

## Before accepting real jobs
- Confirm legal company details, appropriate insurance, customer terms and privacy information.
- Deploy website and worker from the same main revision; check schema and worker health.
- Remove NEXT_PUBLIC_PRELAUNCH=true from Railway build configuration if present; redeploy.
- Verify public HTTPS, quote, callback and booking request on a phone. Use an owner-controlled test contact; mark test records and exclude them from reporting.
- Verify the request appears in CRM, Telegram delivery, owner confirmation, safe weather and diary conflict checks. Never promise an unconfirmed visit.
- Confirm persistent photo storage and database backups, including a restore to a separate database.
- Verify invoice and manual payment workflow; keep manual company records until FreeAgent is connected.

## Optional acquisition integrations
- Verify Google Business Profile, Search Console, Meta business and ad accounts separately.
- Keep live ad spending and external customer communications approval-gated. No advertising budget is authorised by this checklist.

CI results are not evidence of live production integration success. Record actual checks and dates in the launch review before declaring operational readiness.
