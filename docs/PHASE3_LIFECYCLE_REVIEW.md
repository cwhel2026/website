# Phase 3 lifecycle review

Owner-recorded customer agreement is required before job creation. A booking request is not acceptance. Pricing requiring internal approval remains blocked. Jobs preserve the exact property link. Diary conflict checks cannot be bypassed by confirm; job booking requests serialize on scheduling settings. Preparation completion and coating completion are explicit owner actions; coating requires dry surface and safe on-site weather confirmation. Proposals use actual wash completion and seven-day target, with recovery windows for overdue jobs. Draft invoices require completed jobs, are linked and reused. Manual payments require a positive amount and reference, reject overpayment and defer FreeAgent-linked invoices to FreeAgent.

Remaining: real weather validation at booking confirmation, all calendar writers sharing a lock, per-appointment completion for multi-visit jobs, single-visit completion, full CRM stage synchronization, internal price approval UI, payment UI, FreeAgent invoice creation/reconciliation, audit records for all lifecycle actions, end-to-end tests with real PostgreSQL and live integrations. No automatic sending or money movement. No production verification or deployment performed.
